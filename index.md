# Catholic Computer — Computing that answers to people

*Canonical: https://catholiccomputer.com/ · An initiative in formation.*

Catholic Computer explores sovereign computing, decentralized intelligence, and open-source AI, grounded in human dignity and Catholic social teaching. People should be able to keep their lives private, own their data, choose their tools, and exercise their judgment without covert algorithmic manipulation. We want to help make that possible.

## Our starting principles

Christian ethics should shape how technology works, not just the answers it gives. These are the standards we want to translate into design decisions, public research, and working software.

1. **Privacy and ownership** — Keep sensitive information under the user's control. Favor local processing, data minimization, inspectable data flows, and the ability to export or delete what a system holds.
2. **Free will and conscience** — No covert persuasion, engineered dependency, or exploitation of personal vulnerabilities. People must remain free to question, disagree, decide, and disconnect.
3. **Decentralized intelligence** — Explore open models, portable agents, interoperable tools, and computing infrastructure that individuals and communities can run or choose for themselves.
4. **Truth and accountability** — Make claims traceable to sources. Prefer systems whose behavior can be inspected, tested, and explained over systems that only ask to be trusted.
5. **Subsidiarity** — Decisions belong as close to the people they affect as possible. Technology should strengthen families, parishes, and local communities rather than centralizing power away from them.
6. **Stewardship** — Consider the long-term costs of computing: energy, hardware, repairability, and transparent resource costs.

## The domain family

These names describe a possible structure for future work. They are not separate products or services available today.

- **Catholic Computer** (catholiccomputer.com) — The main home for research, software, technical writing, and a community interested in responsible computing informed by Catholic thought.
- **Catholic Compute** (catholiccompute.com) — Open-source systems, local AI, hardware, private infrastructure, and autonomous workflows.
- **Catholic Inference** (catholicinference.com) — Model evaluation, source-grounded research, inference systems, and the study of AI reliability.
- **Christian Compute** (christiancompute.com) — A possible place for engineering and knowledge-sharing across Christian traditions.
- **Christian Inference** (christianinference.com) — A reserved name. Its purpose, if any, will depend on what the work actually needs.
