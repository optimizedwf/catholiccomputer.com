# Catholic Computer — Add your name

*Canonical: https://catholiccomputer.com/signatories · Machine-readable list: https://catholiccomputer.com/signatures.json · Sign-offs are personal endorsements.*

A signature here means one thing only: your personal support for the six principles on the [home page](https://catholiccomputer.com/) — privacy and ownership, freedom of conscience, decentralized intelligence, truth and accountability, subsidiarity, and stewardship. It is not a membership, and it is not an endorsement by any diocese, order, parish, or the Holy See. Clergy and religious are welcome to sign personally, with or without their title, exactly as they choose to be shown.

## How to sign

1. **By email** — write to `signatories@catholiccomputer.com` with your name as you want it shown, your city (optional), and a title if you want one.
2. **By pull request** — add one line to [`signatures.json`](https://github.com/optimizedwf/catholiccomputer.com/blob/main/signatures.json) and open a PR.

One line is all it takes:

```json
{ "name": "Your Name", "city": "Your City", "date": "2026-10-26" }
```

Use initials or a nickname if you prefer. Ask at any time and a listing will be removed or corrected.
